RETROCAR

Privacy policy

Retrocar for iPhone and iPad. Updated October 20, 2026.

Retrocar collects no information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it never connects to the internet.

Who we are

Retrocar is made by MAGMARCAS LTDA, Brazil. Questions about this policy go to support@xl-master.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings on your device: camera, controls and controller bindings, the touch control layout, graphics, audio, free play options and your chosen car and team. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Game controllers, keyboards, mice and vibration are handled by iOS on your device. Retrocar does not use your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or any other personal data.

Information Apple may share

If you have chosen in iOS (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements) to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated usage figures and crash reports for Retrocar that do not identify you. Apple's own privacy policy covers that sharing. We use these reports only to find and fix problems.

Children

Retrocar is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you, and we delete the conversation once it is resolved. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you or to delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If a future version of Retrocar ever needs to collect information, this policy and the App Store privacy details will say so before that version is released.
