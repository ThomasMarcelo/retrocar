Support

Playing

How do I play with a controller?

Retrocar plays best with one, and supports them natively: there is nothing to set up. Pair it in the iOS Settings app under Bluetooth, then press any button in the game. Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and other MFi controllers work, with button prompts that match your controller. The game switches to it straight away and hides the touch controls. Settings > Controls lets you rebind buttons, invert steering or pitch and change the stick dead zone.

The touch controls are in my way

In Settings > Controls, Edit Touch Layout lets you move and resize each control, and Controls opacity makes them see-through.

Can I use a keyboard and mouse?

Yes. On iPad, a connected keyboard or mouse takes over from the touch controls when you use it.

Which camera should I use?

Soccer has an isometric pixel-art view of the whole pitch and a chase view behind your car, picked before each match. Ball cam keeps the ball in view. In Settings > Camera you can tune the chase camera's field of view, distance, height and angle.

Troubleshooting

There is no sound

Retrocar follows the Ring/Silent switch (or Action button) on iPhone, like most games. Turn silent mode off, then check the volumes in Settings > Audio.

The game isn't as smooth as it should be

Retrocar runs at up to 120 frames a second on ProMotion displays. Low Power Mode limits iPhones to 60, and Settings > Graphics has a 60 fps option if you prefer it.

Is my progress saved?

Your settings, controls and chosen car are saved on your device. There are no accounts, so nothing is stored online.

Privacy
